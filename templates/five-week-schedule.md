# Five-week schedule

Students work **20 hours per week**, for example 4 hours a day, Monday to Friday. Adjust to your lab. Meetings in **bold** recur.

"Module" slots are filled from your [module catalog](module-catalog.md), choosing the modules each project needs. Each is taught by a mentor pair using show, do, watch.

## Week 1: Welcome and foundations

| Day | Activities |
|---|---|
| Mon | Welcome and introductions · **Pre-program survey** · Lab tour · Safety training |
| Tue | Read and discuss the project report together · Lab notebooks · Pipetting practice |
| Wed | Solutions and calculations · Module 1 (show, do) |
| Thu | Module 1 (watch) · Module 2 · **Lab group meeting** (observe) |
| Fri | Module 3 · End-of-week reflection: what did you learn? What's confusing? |

## Week 2: Guided experiments

| Day | Activities |
|---|---|
| Mon | Module 4 · Independent calculations |
| Tue | Module 5 · Analyze first data together |
| Wed | Plan the project: which predictions will you test? · Start the budget proposal |
| Thu | Module 6 · **Lab group meeting** · Finish budget proposal |
| Fri | **Subgroup meeting**: informal update · Budget review with mentor |

## Week 3: Independence

| Day | Activities |
|---|---|
| Mon | Budget approved · Place first orders · First independent experiment |
| Tue | Independent experiments · Mentor available for questions |
| Wed | Independent experiments · Analyze results |
| Thu | Troubleshooting: what worked, what didn't, why? · **Lab group meeting** |
| Fri | Independent experiments · **PI check-in** |

## Week 4: Ownership

| Day | Activities |
|---|---|
| Mon | Design your own follow-up experiment |
| Tue | Student-designed experiments |
| Wed | **Site visit** to another university: lab tour, research talks, informal student presentations |
| Thu | Experiments · **Lab group meeting** |
| Fri | **Subgroup meeting**: data and next steps · Update budget tracker |

## Week 5: Results and presentations

| Day | Activities |
|---|---|
| Mon | Final experiments · Begin analysis |
| Tue | Data analysis and figures · Draft presentation |
| Wed | Rehearse presentations; feedback from mentors and returning students · **Post-program survey** |
| Thu | Revise presentation · Clean up bench and notebook |
| Fri | **Final presentations** (families and guests invited) · Celebration |

---

**Returning students** compress weeks 1–2 into a few days, then lead parts of training for new students.
