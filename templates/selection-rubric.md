# Selection rubric

Two reviewers score each application independently, then compare. Discuss any application where scores differ by 3 or more points.

Score each category from 1 to 5.

| Category | 1 · Limited | 3 · Solid | 5 · Exceptional |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Curiosity** (Essay 1) | Generic interest in science | Specific interests or questions | Vivid, personal curiosity; asks their own questions |
| **Vision** (Essay 2) | Little reflection | Some thought about the future | Thoughtful connection between interests and goals, whether settled or still exploring |
| **Commitment to inclusion** (Scenarios) | Responses are brief or miss the point | Kind, reasonable responses | Shows empathy, initiative and awareness of how people are included or excluded |
| **Commitment** (Availability) | Significant conflicts; unlikely to return | Available; might return | Fully available; eager to return |
| **Opportunity** | Already has substantial access to research opportunities | Some access | Little or no current access to research; the program would add the most |

## Reminders for reviewers

- Do **not** weigh writing polish heavily. Look for ideas, not vocabulary.
- Do **not** reward prior research experience. The program is designed for students who don't have it.
- Consider how the cohort fits together: grade levels (so someone returns each year), schools and perspectives.
- Follow your institution's guidance on how eligibility and selection criteria may be applied.
