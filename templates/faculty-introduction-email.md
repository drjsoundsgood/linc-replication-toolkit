# Faculty introduction email

The PI and student draft this together. The **student** usually sends it, copying the PI, so the faculty member hears from the student directly. Adapt it to sound like the student.

Before writing, read the faculty member's lab website and one or two recent papers, so you can mention something specific.

---

**Subject:** Incoming [University] student interested in research in your lab

Dear Professor [Last name],

My name is [Student name], and I'll be starting at [University] this fall, planning to major in [major]. I'm writing because I'm very interested in your lab's research on [topic], especially [something specific from their website or a recent paper].

For the past [number] summers, I've been a researcher in [PI name]'s lab at [Institution] through [Program name], a multi-year research program for high school students. There, I [one or two sentences on what the student did: techniques, the question, results]. [If applicable: This work contributed to a paper in [journal], on which I'm a co-author.]

I'd love to learn more about your research and whether there might be an opportunity to get involved as an undergraduate. Would you have time for a short video call in the next few weeks? [PI name], who is copied here, would be happy to join to introduce me.

I've attached my résumé. Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
[Student name]
[Email]

cc: [PI name]

---

**For the PI:** once the faculty member replies, invite them to attend the student's final presentation virtually.
