## Part 2: Essays

Please answer in your own words. There are no wrong answers. We want to get to know you.

**Essay 1. Why are you interested in science?** (250 words or fewer)
What first made you curious? What questions about the world do you wonder about?

**Essay 2. What do you want to be when you grow up?** (150 words or fewer)
It's fine if you don't know yet, or if you have many ideas. Tell us what you're thinking.

## Part 3: Working together

Science is done in teams. Please write a few sentences (100 words or fewer each) about how you would handle each situation.

**Scenario A.** A new student joins your team halfway through a project. They seem nervous and haven't said much. What would you do?

**Scenario B.** During a team meeting, someone shares an idea and another person laughs it off before it's discussed. What would you do?

**Scenario C.** Describe a time you worked with someone whose background or point of view was different from yours. What did you learn?

*[Programs may substitute or add their own scenarios.]*
