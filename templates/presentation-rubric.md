# Presentation rubric

Use this rubric for peer and mentor feedback on research talks, from subgroup updates to final presentations. Audience members score each presenter and write feedback that is **positive, constructive and specific**.

Listening carefully to a talk, and asking good questions about it, is a skill too. Scoring trains the audience as well as the presenter.

**Presenter:** **Title:** **Reviewer:**

Score each category from 1 to 5.

| Category | What to look for | Score |
|---|---|---|
| **Organization** | Logical order; clear background, question, approach, results and conclusion; the audience can follow the story | |
| **Content** | Technical terms are defined; methods and data are explained clearly; conclusions follow from the data; shows command of the material | |
| **Delivery** | Clear, uncluttered slides; readable figures; speaks clearly to the audience; stays within the time limit | |
| **Response to questions** | Listens to the whole question; answers clearly and confidently; says "I don't know" when appropriate and suggests how to find out | |
