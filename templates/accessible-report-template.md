# Accessible project report template

This report is the high school students' **primary literature**: the first thing they read about their project. Write it so that a motivated 9th grader can understand it. Aim for **2–4 pages**.

**Writing guidelines**
- Define every technical term the first time you use it.
- Prefer short sentences and everyday words.
- Use one or two simple figures.
- Explain *why* the question matters before explaining *how* you studied it.
- Read it aloud, or ask someone outside your field to read it. If they get lost, simplify.

---

## Title
A plain-language title (for example, "Does this RNA stick to this protein?")

**Authors:**

## 1. The big picture (1 paragraph)
What is the biological system? Why should anyone care? Connect it to something familiar, such as health, disease or how cells work.

## 3. What we already know (1–2 paragraphs)
What has been discovered about this system? What's still unknown?

## 4. Our question
One or two sentences stating the question.

## 5. What we did (1–2 paragraphs)
How did you study it (for example databases, software, structure predictions)? Explain each tool in a sentence.

## 6. What we predicted (1–2 paragraphs + figure)
What did you find? Include one clear figure with a plain-language caption.

## 7. How you could test this in the lab
Which experiments could confirm or rule out the predictions? What result would you expect if the prediction is right, and if it's wrong?

## 9. Further reading
