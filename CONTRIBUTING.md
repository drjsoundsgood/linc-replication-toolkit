# Contributing

This toolkit improves as more programs use it. Contributions are welcome:

- **Add your program** to [ADOPTERS.md](ADOPTERS.md).
- **Share what you learned.** Open an issue describing what worked, what didn't, and how you adapted the model for your field or institution.
- **Contribute templates** you've created, such as adapted applications, schedules for other fields, or translated materials for families.
- **Fix errors** or suggest clearer wording through a pull request.

Please don't include names or personal information about students, families or mentors in anything you contribute.
