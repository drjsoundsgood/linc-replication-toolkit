# 11 · Beyond the summer

LiNC's commitment to students doesn't end when the summer does. The program supports students through the high school-to-college transition, which is exactly where many talented students leave STEM.

## During high school

- **College selection:** help students think about which schools offer strong undergraduate research opportunities in their field of interest.
- **Essays:** read and give feedback on college application essays. Students now have a real research experience to write about.
- **Recommendation letters:** the PI and mentors write letters grounded in specific accomplishments.
- **Scholarships and awards:** point students to opportunities where their research experience makes them competitive, and help with applications.

## The senior summer: matching students with college research labs

Students who complete the summer after high school graduation, and who have been admitted to a university with plans to major in STEM, get help finding a research lab there.

1. **Identify faculty.** The student and PI look through faculty at the student's university whose research matches the student's interests.
2. **Draft emails together.** The PI and student write introduction emails to two or three faculty, using [templates/faculty-introduction-email.md](../templates/faculty-introduction-email.md). Writing a cold email to a professor is part of the unwritten curriculum; doing it together makes it teachable.
3. **Video introductions.** Where faculty respond, set up a short video call with the student, PI and faculty member.
4. **Invite them to the final presentation.** Faculty at the student's future university can attend the student's final talk virtually.

Students arrive at college with a research identity, real skills, and a faculty contact.

## After LiNC: alumni support

- Stay in touch through the first years of college. Check in each semester.
- Offer help with coursework where you can. The founding lab tutors alumni through undergraduate courses.
- Help with summer research applications and fellowships.
- Invite alumni back as speakers or near-peer mentors for current students.
- Track outcomes (see [Assessment](13-assessment.md)) with students' consent.
